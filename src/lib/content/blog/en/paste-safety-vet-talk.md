# Paste Safety: the Vet Talk

Videos love big words: "painkiller", "get away from prescription meds", "anti-cancer". We don't write that — here's why, point by point.

## Paste doesn't replace NSAIDs

Head-to-head comparisons show curcumin doesn't match carprofen/meloxicam for pain relief. Its role is an adjunct: comfort and mobility support next to weight control, omega-3s and prescribed treatment. Never drop a working drug for a spice on your own.

## Stop-or-vet-first cases

- NSAIDs, warfarin and other anticoagulants (antiplatelet effect)
- 1–2 weeks before planned surgery
- Gallbladder disease, bile duct obstruction (stimulates bile flow)
- Oxalate stone tendency, severe liver disease
- Puppies under ~12 weeks, pregnancy — no experiments
- Piperine affects liver enzymes (CYP) and can shift levels of other drugs

## Typical side effects — and what's normal

Soft stool, vomiting at the start (dose too high), yellow stains on bowls and fur, yellower stool — the last one is normal, it's turmeric, not a problem.

## What to ask your vet

1. Is the lameness really joints — not ligament, infection, oncology?
2. Does the paste mix with our current drugs?
3. What markers do we watch at 8 weeks (rising, stairs, walks)?

## Studies we stand on

Colitti et al. 2012, PMID 22591841 (CurcuVET 4 mg/kg BID — inflammatory gene-expression shifts); Innes et al. 2003, PMID 12723628 (P54FP extract — subjectively better, force plate unchanged); Comblain et al. 2017, PMID 29262825 (CCOT diet — less manipulation pain); Caterino et al. 2021, PMID 34048452 (CurcuVET + boswellia as adjunct). Mechanism confirmed, clinical effect modest and honest.
