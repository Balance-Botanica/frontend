# Paste Dosing in Jar Shares

No spoons. A daily portion of our paste is a simple share of the jar (about 12 mg curcuminoids per gram, 60 mg per 5 g). Bigger dog, bigger share. The calculator always shows the exact share for your weight — below are the landmarks.

## Portion per day by weight

- **Small, up to 10 kg (≈60–100 mg):** 1/6–1/3 of the 30 g jar
- **Medium, 10–25 kg (≈120–180 mg):** 1/2 of the 30 g jar … 1/6 of the 100 g jar
- **Large, 25+ kg (≈360+ mg):** 1/3 of the 100 g jar … 1/8 of the 250 g jar, split across 2 meals

Read it like this: a medium dog (15 kg) needs 1/2 of the 30 g jar per day, or 1/6 of the 100 g jar, or 1/16 of the 250 g jar, or 1/30 of the 500 g jar — the same portion, different jars.

## How to start

1. Weeks 1–2: half the portion, with fatty food.
2. Stool fine, no vomiting, eats happily — raise to full.
3. Hold at least 6–8 weeks and watch: rising, stairs, walk length.
4. Zero change by week 8 — raising the dose forever makes no sense, see your vet.

## Jars in days (small dog, ~60 mg/day)

- **30 g** — ~6 days: taste and stomach test
- **100 g** — ~20 days: the first weeks
- **250 g** — ~50 days: half of the assessment course
- **500 g** — ~100 days: full course + subscription

Medium and large dogs will go through jars faster: the exact share and days for your dog are in the calculator on the homepage.
