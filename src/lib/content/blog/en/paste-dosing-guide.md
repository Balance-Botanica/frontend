# Paste Dosing in Spoons

Both videos agree: the dose is small, the start is slow. We merged their schemes with our label (**~60 mg curcuminoids per tsp**):

## Spoons per day (with food)

- **Small, up to 10 kg:** 1/4–1/2 tsp (15–30 mg)
- **Medium, 10–25 kg:** 1/2–1 tsp (30–60 mg)
- **Large, 25+ kg:** 1–2 tsp (60–120 mg), split across 2 meals

For reference, Lolahemp suggests 1/4 tsp for small, 1/2 for medium, 3/4–1 for large dogs daily — the same volumes, except ours has known milligrams inside.

## How to start

1. Weeks 1–2: half the target dose, with fatty food.
2. Stool fine, no vomiting, eats happily — raise to full.
3. Hold at least 6–8 weeks and watch: rising, stairs, walk length.
4. Zero change by week 8 — raising the dose forever makes no sense, see your vet.

## Jars in days (medium dog, 1 tsp/day)

- **TRIAL 30 g** (~6 tsp) — taste and stomach test
- **WEEK 100 g** (~20 tsp) — the first weeks
- **HALF 250 g** (~50 tsp) — half of the assessment course
- **MONTH 500 g** — full month + subscription

Small dogs stretch jars longer, large dogs shorter: exact days for your dog are in the calculator on the homepage.
